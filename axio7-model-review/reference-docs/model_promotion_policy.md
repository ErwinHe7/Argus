# Model promotion policy

Promotion is a risk decision, not an accuracy contest. A model must beat simple directional, clock-time, momentum and mean-reversion baselines on untouched data; produce calibrated probabilities; have a positive cost-adjusted expected value with a positive block-bootstrap lower bound; remain stable across regimes and parameter perturbations; and pass deterministic replay, leakage, risk, OMS and ledger tests.

`REJECTED_NO_DEPLOYABLE_EDGE` is a complete and acceptable research result. No rejected model may create a paper order. Paper promotion still leaves the global kill switch active until explicitly reviewed.
