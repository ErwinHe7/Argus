# IBKR public integration handoff

Prepared 2026-09-11. This is an implementation and review brief, not an application
submission, broker approval, or representation that Leo can trade live accounts.

## Existing product to demonstrate

- Website: <https://leo.axio7.workers.dev/#/brokers>.
- Google sign-in establishes the Leo owner. Each user's brokerage authentication
  and consent are separate.
- Users select fixed mean-reversion or momentum rules, inspect their evidence,
  pair their own connector, and review a paper mandate.
- The local Rust runner persists intents, reconciles account/order state, enforces
  an authorization lease and position/loss limits, and reports evidence to Leo.
- Client Portal execution is an experimental opt-in with an exact paper account
  and the execution session's `isPaper: true` proof. TWS inspection is read-only.
- No live-money operation is released. Broker-backed paper lifecycle acceptance
  has not been completed. Injected test results are not broker evidence.

The adapter and local recovery contract are in
[IBKR.md](../../connectors/strategy/adapter/IBKR.md). Product status and test
boundaries are in [BROKER-CONNECTIONS.md](BROKER-CONNECTIONS.md).

## Broker onboarding still required

IBKR's [third-party registration process](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/web-api/authentication/oauth-1a/third-party-oauth/registration-process)
applies to platforms serving users outside their organization, including automatic
trading products. It requests a completed site and finalized workflow or an
integration proof of concept. The listed initial contact is
`api-solutions@interactivebrokers.com`.

The owner must supply the legal business identity, intended customer regions,
final service/pricing description and the registrations or legal opinion IBKR
requires for the intended offering. Those facts cannot be inferred from a Google
login or generated by the implementation. IBKR then reviews the application and,
if approved, provides agreement and public-key/callback instructions. A consumer
key is not available just by installing Gateway.

No contact email was sent and no agreement was accepted during this work. The
current documentation estimates three successive stages of 2–3, 3–6 and 3–5
weeks, with variable timing. These are broker estimates, not Leo delivery dates.

## Local testing is a separate route

Client Portal Gateway requires an eligible funded IBKR Pro account and browser
authentication on the same machine as the gateway/API client. It requires daily
reauthentication and does not support automated login. A VPS does not remove this
requirement. See the official [installation guide](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/web-api/authentication/cpgw/installation-authentication),
[same-machine limitations](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/web-api/authentication/cpgw/limitations-of-the-client-portal-gateway)
and [authentication FAQ](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/docs/web-api/authentication/cpgw/client-portal-gateway-faq).

The owner's installed local gateway is reachable by the pinned-certificate
transport, but its read-only authentication status was unavailable on this check.
The browser reported an untrusted local certificate; that interstitial was not
bypassed. No authenticated account, mandate or order was inferred from a listening
port. Complete broker sign-in, certificate setup and the paper acceptance cycle
before describing the local installation as connected.
