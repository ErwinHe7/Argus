# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

English-speaking adults who want two distinct tools in one coherent destination: evidence-first, impersonal market research and a private symbolic-reflection practice. They may arrive on mobile or desktop and should be able to understand the product, open either app, and complete a useful first session without an account or payment.

## Product Purpose

AXIO7 is a unified web product for inspecting evidence and reflecting on uncertainty. The public homepage introduces the system through an original cinematic cosmic experience, then routes visitors into the Research workspace or the Astrology app. Research remains reproducible and fail-closed. Astrology provides prompts for reflection and memory, never market evidence, causal claims, financial forecasts, investment advice, or trade execution. The first release must run locally and on a zero-cost public preview before paid infrastructure is introduced.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, rigorous, reflective. The experience should feel expansive and emotionally resonant without sacrificing clarity, trust, or restraint inside the working apps.

## Anti-references

Do not clone another creator's code, assets, name, or exact composition. Avoid casino-like trading dashboards, generic neon AI landing pages, fake scientific astrology, fortune-telling certainty, financial mysticism, autoplay audio, and visual effects that make the tools unreadable or inaccessible.

## Design Principles

1. Earn the spectacle: use one memorable cosmic first impression, then make every working surface calm and task-focused.
2. Keep boundaries visible: separate symbolic reflection from evidence, prediction, and market research in both copy and interaction.
3. Useful before signup: the homepage, Research entry, and a complete Astrology reflection must work for guests without paid services.
4. Show provenance and limits: preserve sources, uncertainty, privacy choices, and honest capability states.
5. One system, two modes: share navigation, typography, accessibility, and account conventions while allowing Research and Astrology to retain distinct atmospheres.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks and labels, 44px touch targets, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. The cosmic scene must degrade to a still visual when motion is reduced or canvas is unavailable. No audio may start automatically.
