# Astra's Pick — private founder brief

Working product brief, not public marketing or a statement of traction.

## Problem and users

Independent investors can inspect a chart, model an option, and record an idea
in separate tools without preserving the reasoning between them. Astra's Pick
aims to make that sequence easier for English-speaking self-directed users:
understand the market, test assumptions, and keep a reviewable plan.

## Product workflow

Choose a stock or benchmark, analyze its chart and descriptive reading, compare
price/IV/time scenarios for supported stock options, then save a thesis and
invalidation condition. Bitcoin and Ethereum charts provide a separate route
into a local plan. The product supports a user's decision; it does not execute
orders or claim calibrated directional probabilities.

## Current capabilities

The implemented interface has thirteen tools, eight archived IBKR instruments,
six supported option symbols, 12,960 precomputed conditional option valuations,
and browser-local notes, plans, and calendar entries with JSON export. Python
produces descriptive trend/momentum readings. The stock data is through
September 4, 2026. Crypto is a TradingView-hosted Coinbase chart embed, not a
local crypto feed. The original Leo site, app, and membership flow are preserved.

Current gaps include a refreshing licensed market-data pipeline, independently
timestamped IV history and contract coverage, calibrated forecast validation,
cloud record synchronization, and verified brokerage execution records. The
current interface and external chart embed do not establish those capabilities.

## Paid-value hypotheses to test

Users may pay for reliable refresh and coverage, saved cross-device workflows,
clear assumption comparisons, and a durable history of plans and reviews.
These are hypotheses, not shipped promises. Test which capability earns repeat
use and willingness to pay before adding subscriptions or claiming a price
advantage. Data entitlements, latency, source quality, and operating cost must
support any paid offer.

## Validation and investor evidence

Measure the proportion of visitors who select a market and open a chart;
chart-to-scenario and scenario-to-plan completion; time to the first saved plan;
returning users who review a plan; seven- and thirty-day cohort retention;
source/cutoff comprehension; and export or synchronization demand. Pair these
with interviews about the decision the user completed and the tools they used
beforehand. Paid pilots can test conversion and renewal only after the proposed
capability exists.

Track source freshness, failed data checks, contract coverage, support burden,
data/vendor cost per active user, and gross margin alongside adoption. Keep
analysis quality, UX completion, and investment performance separate; verified
performance requires a causal dated record and appropriate validation. No user
count, revenue, retention, partner commitment, or investment return is asserted
in this brief.
