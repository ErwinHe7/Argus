# Model atlas

Mode: Operate / Read. The atlas extends the established LEO research workspace.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the frozen Argus model inspectable through linked charts and exact
artifact provenance. The selected model and its regimes lead exploration.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit LEO's aubergine surfaces, candle-gold selection, lilac model
geometry, Inter typography, Lion identity, and ruled data tables.

STORY: Choose a model, select a regime, inspect transitions and state-count
selection, then read kernel settings and lineage. The pipeline is a disclosure
so the charts remain the main working surface.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Retained navigation, Model atlas heading, concise parameter
status, nine model selectors, then the regime chart and selected readout.

FORM: Local extension within the existing research workspace. No new visual
identity or dashboard redesign.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Verification

Desktop and 390px phone inspection; linked regime selection, model deep links,
pipeline disclosure, and 12-to-11 regime switching are release checks. Existing
DESIGN.md remains authoritative. No new raster assets are introduced.

## Final review · October 22, 2026

Verdict: **ship** for the rendered regime views and two bounded accessibility
corrections. The independent review first returned **fix**: the state-count score
chart needed a native data table, and abbreviated lineage identifiers needed a
keyboard-accessible, selectable full-text disclosure. Candidate scores now
provides column and row headers, eligibility, selection, and numerical scores;
Full artifact identifiers exposes the hashes, commit, and artifact path with
wrapping text. Both findings are resolved in source and captured evidence. This
is not a whole-product audit.

The atlas renders nine frozen candidates, with a nine-column picker at 1200px
and above and three columns at 700px and below. The pipeline disclosure and
linked regime chart, selected readout, and regime table preserve the established
workspace hierarchy. Native regime, transition, and candidate-score tables
provide the numerical reading path. Browser checks at 1280px and 390px found no
page overflow or runtime errors and exercised keyboard regime selection and
switching from 12 to 11 regimes.
