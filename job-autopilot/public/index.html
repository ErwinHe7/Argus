Job Autopilot

Upload your resume and the agent finds matching jobs on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby and fills in the applications for you. It submits only when it can answer every required question truthfully from your resume and the facts you give it.

4. Find jobs

Companies to search

One per line as greenhouse:slug, lever:slug or ashby:slug. The slug is the company part of the job board link, e.g. jobs.lever.co/palantir.

Or paste job links

Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby links, one per line. Find them on LinkedIn or company career pages.
