# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Curious Gen Z and millennial women arriving on a phone during a quiet evening, commute, or pause between other demands. They want composed orientation to Argus before choosing whether to learn more or explore the standalone Demo.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Argus as an intimate, evidence-aware frontend for an existing Webull brokerage relationship and provide a disposable, clearly synthetic product demonstration. The website must never imply that its Demo accounts, prices, communities, balances, or transactions are real.

## Authority Model

Webull is authoritative for brokerage accounts, capabilities, restrictions, balances, positions, and orders. Argus is authoritative for ritual, editorial, community, preference, and privacy state. Supabase owns Argus identity, application data, server compute, durable jobs, and retained object storage.

Cloudflare Pages serves only static landing and standalone Demo assets. It hosts no Pages Functions, `_worker.js`, Worker route, Worker binding, Queue, Cron, R2, authentication, or stateful request handling. If the waitlist is retained, the static form calls a narrowly scoped Supabase Edge Function; preview submissions remain disabled unless explicitly configured for the shared sandbox.

The standalone Demo is synthetic, memory-only, auth-free, provider-free, credential-free, and network-isolated. It does not call Supabase, Webull, Auth0, Neon, Cloudflare compute, or any production API.

## Brand Personality

Mystic, discerning, intimate. The site should feel like a candlelit observatory: calm enough to linger in, precise enough to trust, and never breathless or sales-driven.

## Anti-references

- Generic finance dashboards, navy-and-green brokerage styling, or conversion-first SaaS pages.
- Juvenile horoscope novelty, occult clip-art collage, or hidden disclosure language.
- Urgency, scarcity, guarantees, romance, or intimacy used to influence a financial decision or waitlist submission.
- Any implication that Argus opens, funds, holds, or autonomously manages a brokerage account.
- Any implication that Demo prices, portfolios, communities, accounts, or transactions are live.

## Design Principles

- Let the standalone Demo be the primary product illustration while preserving its independent synthetic boundary.
- Lead with Daily Ritual, then keep Asset Codex evidence, uncertainty, Coven and Grimoire context, and Webull authority legible.
- Keep disclosures, privacy terms, Retry, Reset, Open Demo, and the waitlist usable without pressure or hidden states.
- Preserve a semantic, no-framework document whose essential meaning remains readable without JavaScript.
- Rowan cannot issue financial commands and remains non-advisory and non-coercive wherever his language appears.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, resilient text reflow, descriptive Demo labeling, and minimum 44px touch targets. Never rely on color alone, trap focus in the Demo, or require the Demo to understand the waitlist and privacy notice.
