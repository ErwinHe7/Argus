# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Curious Gen Z and millennial women arriving on a phone during a quiet evening, commute, or pause between other demands. They want composed orientation to Ritual Investing before deciding whether to explore a simulated product and leave an email for a future launch contact.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Ritual Investing as an intimate, evidence-aware investing companion; let visitors explore the real product language in a disposable auth-free Demo; and offer a minimal, privacy-respecting provisional waitlist. Success means the mystical frame invites curiosity while simulated state, uncertainty, privacy, and non-advice boundaries remain unmistakable.

## Brand Personality

Mystic, discerning, intimate. The site should feel like a candlelit observatory: calm enough to linger in, precise enough to trust, and never breathless or sales-driven.

## Anti-references

- Generic finance dashboards, navy-and-green brokerage styling, or conversion-first SaaS pages.
- Juvenile horoscope novelty, occult clip-art collage, or hidden disclosure language.
- Gradient text, glass surfaces, decorative grids, card-grid feature inventories, oversized shadows, and ornamental side stripes.
- Urgency, scarcity, guarantees, romance, or intimacy used to influence a financial decision or waitlist submission.
- Any implication that Demo prices, portfolios, communities, accounts, or transactions are real.

## Design Principles

- Let the embedded real Demo be the primary product image; supporting art establishes atmosphere rather than replacing the product.
- Use the committed lunar lilac, aubergine, and rare candle-gold palette with Libre Baskerville for editorial moments and Inter for controls and evidence.
- Lead with a Daily Ritual, then keep Asset Codex evidence, uncertainty, Coven and Grimoire context, and simulated portfolio framing close.
- Make disclosures, privacy terms, Retry, Reset, Open Demo, and the waitlist usable without pressure or hidden states.
- Preserve a semantic, no-framework document whose essential meaning remains readable without JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, resilient text reflow, descriptive iframe labeling, and minimum 44px touch targets. Never rely on color alone, trap focus in the Demo, or require the Demo to understand the waitlist and privacy notice.
